### Opportunities to investigate

- Value-for-money (4.8) is the only visible category below 5.0; pricing and perceived value should be benchmarked.
- Host reservation approval is 88%; private cancellation/rejection reasons could reveal an operational opportunity.
- One guest explicitly requested an insect screen for the entrance. This is a concrete, low-cost improvement to track.
- Access slope, summer humidity/insects, non-drinkable tap water, and bottled-gas supply are disclosed constraints. Guest communication should set expectations clearly.
- With only 9 public reviews, increasing review volume is likely important for strengthening conversion and confidence.

These are hypotheses for investigation, not claims about their effect on bookings.
